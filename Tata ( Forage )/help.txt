----------------------------------------------------------------------------

InvoiceNo
A unique invoice (bill) number.
Each invoice represents a transaction.
If it starts with “C”, it means Credit Note → a cancelled order or return.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------


StockCode
A unique code for each product/item.
Example: “85123A” = a specific product.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------


Description
The name of the product.
Example: “White Ceramic Mug”
----------------------------------------------------------------------------


Quantity
Number of units bought in that invoice line.
Example: 6 = customer bought 6 pieces of the product.
Negative values = product returned.

    Example:

            A quantity of 6 means:
            Customer bought 6 units

            A quantity of –6 means:
            Customer returned 6 units
----------------------------------------------------------------------------



UnitPrice
Price of one unit of the product.
Example: UnitPrice = 2.55 (means one piece costs £2.55)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------


CustomerID
Unique ID for each customer.
Used to:
Identify repeat customers
Build customer profiles
Group customers by spend
----------------------------------------------------------------------------


Country
Country where the order was placed.
Used for:
Regional revenue comparison
Expansion analysis
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
